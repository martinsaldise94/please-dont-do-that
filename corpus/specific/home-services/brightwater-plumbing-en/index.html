What We Fix

Emergency Plumbing

  • Burst and leaking pipes (copper, plastic, pushfit)
  • Blocked drains and toilets — internal
  • Overflowing tanks and cisterns
  • No hot water — cylinder and combi faults
  • Frozen pipe thawing (winter call-outs)
  • Leak detection under floors and behind tiles
  • Stopcock replacement and isolation

Boiler & Heating

  • Combi boiler faults — Worcester Bosch, Vaillant, Baxi, Ideal
  • Annual boiler service (Gas Safe certified)
  • Landlord Gas Safety Certificates (CP12)
  • Radiator bleeding, balancing, and replacement
  • Thermostatic radiator valve (TRV) replacement
  • Magnetic system filters (Magnaclean / Fernox)
  • Central heating power flush

Planned Work

  • Bathroom suite installation (supply and fit)
  • Shower tray and enclosure installation
  • Outdoor tap supply (single or double)
  • Kitchen appliance connection (dishwasher, washing machine)
  • Full bathroom re-pipe
  • Underfloor heating (wet system) servicing
  • Pressure testing (new builds and extensions)

Phil Okafor — Lead Engineer

Phil completed his plumbing and heating NVQ Level 3 at City of Bristol College in 2005 and worked as a gas engineer for Severn Trent Property Services between 2005 and 2013, servicing boilers across 2,400 managed properties in Avon and Somerset.

He set up Brightwater Plumbing in January 2013, initially as a sole trader and converting to a limited company (Brightwater Plumbing & Heating Ltd, Company No. 09184723) in 2017. He took on apprentice Marcus Threlfall in 2022; Marcus passed his ACS assessments in October 2024 and now assists on all non-gas work independently.

Phil services approximately 180 boilers per year and has fitted over 60 complete bathroom suites in properties across BS6–BS8. He is familiar with the older Victorian pipe layouts common in Redland and Clifton terraces, including lead-to-copper transition work.

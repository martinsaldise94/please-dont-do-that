Specialty Coffee · Roasted in Inverness, Scotland

Single-origin beans, roasted to order in the Highlands

Founded by Alasdair Munro in 2014, Highland Coffee Roasters sources micro-lot beans directly from producers in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Honduras. We roast on Tuesdays and Thursdays on our 15kg Probat drum roaster in our Crown Avenue warehouse — then ship the same week.

☕

Why we started — and why it still matters

Alasdair Munro spent eight years as a barista in Edinburgh before moving north with his partner Fiona in 2012. Frustrated that every specialty roaster he admired was based in London or Manchester, he imported a second-hand Probat L12 roaster, leased a unit on Crown Avenue, and started roasting for local cafés in Inverness and Nairn.

Today HCR supplies seventeen independent cafés across the Highlands and Islands, runs a weekly subscription service with just over 400 household subscribers, and makes two direct-trade sourcing trips a year — one to the Sidama zone of Ethiopia (October) and one to Antioquia, Colombia (March).

Roasting stays in-house. We don't outsource, we don't co-roast, and we don't sell anything we haven't personally cupped. Our minimum lot size is 30kg green, which means we work with small farms and cooperatives who rarely get access to the UK specialty market.

The Roastery

We roast at Unit 4, Crown Avenue Industrial Estate, Inverness IV3 8TH. The roastery is open for collection on roast days (Tuesday and Thursday, 10am–4pm) — just mention you're collecting when you place your order and we'll hold it for you.

We also run a monthly public cupping session on the first Saturday of each month at 10am. Tickets are £8 and available through our website. Capacity is limited to 12 people per session.

Wholesale enquiries: email wholesale@highlandcoffeeroasters.co.uk or call Alasdair directly on 01463 728 400.

Shipping rates

UK mainland: £2.95 (Tracked 48) or £4.50 (Tracked 24). Free on orders over £30. Scottish Highlands & Islands: £4.50 flat. Sorry, no international shipping yet.

When will my order ship?

We only ship fresh-roasted coffee. Orders placed before Monday 5pm dispatch Tuesday. Orders placed before Wednesday 5pm dispatch Thursday. Other orders wait for the next roast day.

Roast date guarantee

Every bag is labelled with its roast date. We never ship anything older than 10 days from roast. If your bag arrives and the roast date is more than 10 days ago, email us and we'll replace it.

We build fast, focused websites for independent businesses

Threadline is a three-person web studio based in Edinburgh. We design and develop in-house — no white-labelling, no outsourced dev, no account managers who've never touched a line of code.

We take on eight to ten projects per year. Most are for Scottish SMEs, hospitality businesses, and B2B services companies that need a site that actually converts — not just looks good in a Figma presentation. Project minimum is £3,500.

Selected Work — 2023–2025

01
Bòrd Restaurant Group
Brand site + booking integration
2024 · Edinburgh

Redesign of a three-restaurant Edinburgh group's web presence after a rebrand

Bòrd's previous site ran on a legacy WordPress theme that loaded in 8+ seconds on mobile and had a bounce rate above 74%. Orla led a visual direction built around the group's new colour palette and custom typeface. Callum built the Next.js site with Sanity for menu management and integrated directly with their ResDiary booking system via API. Priya rewrote all copy across the three restaurant sub-sites.

Stack: Next.js 14, Sanity, Tailwind CSS, ResDiary API, Vercel

LCP reduced from 8.2s → 1.4s Bounce rate 74% → 41% Online bookings +38% in first quarter
02
Cairn Capital Advisory
B2B marketing site
2024 · Edinburgh / London

New marketing site for an independent financial advisory firm regulated by the FCA

Cairn needed a site that conveyed credibility to institutional clients while complying with FCA financial promotion rules. Priya mapped content requirements to FCA COBS 4 guidelines before a single design was produced. Orla designed around a restrained editorial aesthetic — no stock imagery, no gradient blobs. Callum built the site with static generation and a Sanity-backed news section.

Stack: Next.js, Sanity, Framer Motion, Vercel; compliance review by Cairn's in-house legal team

Zero compliance revisions after final legal review Ranked #3 Google UK for target keyword within 90 days
03
The Leith Smoot Co.
E-commerce site + brand identity
2023 · Edinburgh

Full identity and Shopify build for a small-batch soap and skincare maker in Leith

The founders came to us with a product, a postal address, and a logo they'd made in Canva. Priya ran a two-day brand workshop to establish tone, positioning, and name hierarchy. Orla developed a complete visual identity — wordmark, label system, photography direction — before building the Figma prototype. Callum implemented on Shopify with a custom Liquid theme, subscription management via Recharge, and a Klaviyo email sequence.

Stack: Shopify, custom Liquid theme, Recharge subscriptions, Klaviyo

£22,400 revenue in first 90 days post-launch Stocked in 3 Edinburgh independent retailers within 6 months
04
Greenvale Renewables
Lead-generation site
2023 · Stirling

B2B lead-gen site for a Stirlingshire solar and heat pump installer targeting commercial clients

Greenvale's previous site generated one or two qualified enquiries a month. Callum rebuilt on Next.js with schema markup targeting commercial solar and heat pump searches across the Central Belt. Orla simplified the visual language and introduced a case study section. We ran three rounds of A/B testing on the contact form with PostHog before confirming the final version.

Stack: Next.js, TypeScript, PostHog (A/B testing), Airtable (enquiry CRM), Vercel

Qualified enquiries: 2/mo → 14/mo within 4 months 2 enterprise contracts (£80k+ combined) attributed to organic search within 6 months

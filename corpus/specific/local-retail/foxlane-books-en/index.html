A shop run by book people, for book people

Fox Lane Books started as a box of crime novels on a trestle table at the Harrogate Antiques Fair. Owner Peter Ashworth — a former secondary-school English teacher from Knaresborough — spent two years hunting for the right premises before signing the lease on the Cheltenham Crescent shop in November 2009.

Today we carry around 40,000 titles across three connected rooms. We specialise in Yorkshire local history, vintage crime fiction, and natural history, but you'll also find healthy shelves of literature, cookery, art, and children's classics. Everything is priced fairly: most paperbacks between £1.50 and £4.50, hardbacks from £3.

Meet the Stylists

All our stylists are fully qualified with NVQ Level 3 in Hairdressing or equivalent and have specialist training in Afro & textured hair.

CO
Chidinma Okonkwo
Founder & Lead Stylist — NVQ Level 3, Trichology Diploma
Born in Lagos and trained in London at the Sassoon Academy, Chidinma opened the studio in 2016 after 10 years working in Brixton and Hackney. She specialises in sisterlocks, Senegalese twists, and scalp health consultations. She regularly travels to Lagos to stay current with the latest protective-style techniques.
AB
Abena Boateng
Senior Stylist — NVQ Level 3, Wig Construction Certificate
Originally from Kumasi, Ghana, Abena trained at the London School of Beauty & Make-Up and has been with us since 2019. She is our wig and weave specialist, offering bespoke human-hair wig units and seamless installs. She also runs our monthly wig maintenance clinic on the first Saturday of each month.
FO
Funmi Odebode
Beauty Therapist — VTCT Level 3, Threading Certificate
Funmi joined in 2021 and looks after our beauty therapy side — waxing, threading, facials, and gel nails. She trained at South Thames College right here in Lewisham and has a particular interest in melanin-rich skin care, using predominantly Fenty Skin and SheaMoisture products.

Booking Policies

Deposits

A £20 non-refundable deposit is taken at booking via our online system or bank transfer. It is deducted from your total on the day.

Cancellations

Please give at least 48 hours notice to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations forfeit the deposit. No-shows will be charged 50% of the service fee.

Children

Children under 12 are welcome for braiding appointments on Saturday mornings (9am–12pm only). We ask that a parent or guardian remains on-site.

Consultation

First-time clients for sisterlocks or scalp treatments must book a free 20-minute phone consultation before their appointment.

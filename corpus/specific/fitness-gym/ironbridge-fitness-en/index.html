Not a gym. A studio.

Ironbridge Fitness opened in September 2021 in a 3,200 sq ft industrial unit on Stafford Park. Founder Craig Hennessey spent eleven years coaching at Shrewsbury Athletic Club and three years running the strength programme at a CrossFit affiliate in Wolverhampton before deciding Telford needed a dedicated strength and conditioning facility that wasn't also trying to be a spa.

The studio has twelve squat racks, a full set of Eleiko barbells and bumper plates, a 40-metre turf sprint track, and eight Concept2 rowing machines. No rowing machines running daytime TV. No up-sell to a smoothie bar. Just a serious place to get stronger.

All classes are capped at 10 people. All coaches hold at least a Level 3 Personal Training qualification plus UKSCA accreditation. Craig coaches the 6 am and 7 pm sessions personally on weekdays.

Meet Your Physiotherapists

All practitioners are registered with the Health & Care Professions Council (HCPC) and members of the Chartered Society of Physiotherapy (CSP).

EC
Dr Emma Clarke
MCSP · BSc (Hons) Physiotherapy · HCPC Reg. PH123456
Emma completed her physiotherapy degree at the University of Leeds in 2007 and has 17 years of clinical experience. She specialises in lumbar spine disorders and ACL rehabilitation, and is a qualified McKenzie Method practitioner.
DM
Diego Martínez
MCSP · MSc Musculoskeletal Physiotherapy · HCPC Reg. PH234567
Originally from Bilbao, Diego trained at King's College London and joined Clarke & Martínez in 2014. He specialises in sports soft-tissue injuries, dry needling, and post-operative shoulder rehabilitation. He is pitch-side physiotherapist for Headingley RUFC.
SP
Simone Patel
MCSP · BSc Physiotherapy · HCPC Reg. PH345678
Simone joined the practice in 2019 after working in the Leeds Teaching Hospitals NHS Trust MSK outpatients department. She has a particular interest in persistent pain conditions and vestibular rehabilitation following concussion.

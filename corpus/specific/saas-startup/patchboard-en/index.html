The Problem

Generic booking software wasn't built for how tattoo studios actually work

Before Patchboard Before

  • DMs and emails scattered across Instagram, WhatsApp, and Gmail
  • Deposit collected over bank transfer, manually tracked in a spreadsheet
  • No way to tell clients "this flash piece is taken" — double-booking happens
  • Touch-up appointments live in a separate notebook
  • Generic reminder texts that say "salon appointment" and confuse clients
  • Artists can't see each other's availability without asking

With Patchboard After

  • One inbox for all enquiries, tagged by type (custom / flash / touch-up)
  • Deposit links auto-generated via Stripe, logged against the booking
  • Flash sheet uploaded once — clients book directly, marked sold when full
  • Touch-up queue linked to the original appointment automatically
  • Reminders say "your appointment with Jade at Void & Veil, Thursday 3pm" — no salon vibes
  • Shared studio calendar, per-artist colour coded
Features

Built for how tattooers actually book clients

Every feature exists because a studio owner asked for it. Nothing ported over from a hair salon product.

📄

Flash Sheet Booking

Upload your flash sheet as an image or PDF. Each design gets its own bookable slot. When it's claimed, it's marked sold automatically — no more double-booking the same flash.

Tattoo studio-specific
💰

Stripe Deposit Collection

Set your deposit amount per booking type (e.g., £50 for custom pieces, £20 for flash). Clients pay at booking time via Stripe. Deposits are deducted from the final invoice automatically.

🔄

Touch-Up Queue

Completed appointments get a "Schedule touch-up" button. The touch-up booking is linked to the original session, so you always know what was done and when. Set your own free touch-up window (e.g., 3 months).

Tattoo studio-specific
🎨

Artist Availability, Per Chair

Each artist sets their own hours. Clients choose an artist first, then see only that artist's open slots. No more "is Jade free on Thursday" messages.

📨

Studio-Branded Reminders

Reminder messages say your studio name, your artist's name, and exactly what the appointment is. Templates built for tattooing — no "your beauty appointment" wording.

🗂️

Enquiry Triage Inbox

All incoming booking requests land in one inbox, tagged by type. Snooze an enquiry until you're back from your guest spot. Assign it to the right artist in one click.

Our Story

We built this because we needed it ourselves

Nadia Osei ran Void & Veil Tattoo in Shoreditch for four years. By 2023 she was spending six hours a week on admin — chasing deposits over bank transfer, manually updating a shared Google Calendar, answering "is slot X still available?" on Instagram at 11pm. She tried three different booking platforms. All of them were built for spas or barber shops and treated tattoo appointments like haircuts: a fixed duration, an interchangeable service, no understanding of custom work.

She met Callum Rennie — a developer and ex-tattooist who had apprenticed in Glasgow before moving into software — at a design meet-up in 2023. They spent three months talking to 40 studio owners across the UK and built the first version of Patchboard over the following winter. Void & Veil became the first paying customer in February 2024. By the end of that year, 180 UK studios were using it.

Patchboard is self-funded, based in London, and we intend to keep it that way. We don't want to raise VC money and be told to expand into nail salons.

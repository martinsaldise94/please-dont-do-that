Meet Your Tutors

Our tutors are subject specialists, not generalists. Every tutor has taught their subject for at least five years.

PP

Priya Patel

BSc Mathematics (First Class), University of Cambridge · QTS

Priya taught A-Level Maths and Further Maths at Clifton College for seven years before joining Bright Sparks full-time in 2022. She holds a distinction in the Cambridge CELTA and has mentored over 200 students through GCSE and A-Level examinations. In 2025, 18 of her 19 A-Level Maths students achieved an A or A*.

OG

Owen Griffiths

MA English Literature, University of Exeter · PGCE Secondary English

Owen spent six years as Head of English at Redland High School before moving to independent tutoring in 2021. He specialises in examination technique and creative writing, and has helped twelve students gain places at Oxford and Cambridge to read English. Owen is also a published short-story writer and a First Assessor for the AQA GCSE English Literature paper.

AM

Dr Anya Mehta

PhD Organic Chemistry, University of Bristol · PGCE Science

Anya completed her doctorate at the University of Bristol in 2016 and spent four years as a research chemist at Syngenta before retraining as a teacher. She joined Bright Sparks in 2020 and leads all chemistry and combined science provision. Anya's students achieved a 100% pass rate in A-Level Chemistry in both 2024 and 2025.
